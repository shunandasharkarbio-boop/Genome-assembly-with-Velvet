## 📊 MultiQC Summary

**MultiQC** combines FastQC reports into a single interactive report, providing an overview of sequencing quality. Key metrics reviewed include:

- **General Statistics** – Read length and basic sequencing information.
- **Sequence Quality** – Per-base quality scores across reads.
- **GC Content** – Distribution of GC content to identify potential biases.
- **Per Base N Content** – Detects ambiguous bases (`N`) that may require trimming.
- **k-mer Content** – Identifies overrepresented sequences that may indicate adapter contamination or other technical artifacts.
